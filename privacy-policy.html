Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Content Auto Post uses information when an authorized user connects their Facebook account and Facebook Pages.

1. About Content Auto Post

Content Auto Post is a private content publishing and social media management tool. It helps authorized users manage and publish content to Facebook Pages they control.

2. Information We Access

When a user authorizes the application through Facebook, Content Auto Post may access information necessary to identify and manage Facebook Pages that the user has permission to manage.

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, this may include:

  • A list of Facebook Pages the user manages.
  • Basic information about those Pages.
  • Content published on those Pages.
  • Permissions necessary to create and manage Page posts.

3. Facebook Permissions

Content Auto Post may request the following Facebook permissions:

  • pages_show_list – to identify Pages managed by the user.
  • pages_read_engagement – to access content and engagement information for authorized Pages.
  • pages_manage_posts – to create and manage posts on authorized Pages.

4. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Facebook is used only to provide content publishing and Page management functionality requested by the authorized user.

Content Auto Post does not sell Facebook user data or Page data to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade information obtained through Facebook. Information is only processed as necessary to operate the application's publishing and Page management functions.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality or to comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke Content Auto Post's access at any time through their Facebook settings and connected business integrations.

8. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of Content Auto Post.

Instructions are available on our User Data Deletion Instructions page.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials and information used by the application from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality, permissions, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
